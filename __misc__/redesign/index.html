Redesign concepts_

All four keep the grid background and the cyan primary. Corners are sharp everywhere.

01

Neo-Brutal Cyan

Thick ink borders, hard offset shadows and buttons that press in. Adds yellow and pink accents. Heavy 40px grid on cream paper.
02

Blueprint / Drafting Sheet

The site as an engineering drawing: cyan graph paper, dimension lines, callouts and a title block. Has a light "drafting" mode and a navy "blueprint" mode.
03

Swiss Grid / Editorial

The background grid is the layout grid. Huge type, cyan rows that flood in on hover, and a cell that snaps to the cursor.
04

Terminal / Tiling WM

Dark mode built from tiled panes. A spotlight lights up the grid around the cursor, keys 1–4 jump between sections, and experience reads as a git log.

Minimal & non-technical friendly_

Plain-language copy, few navigation choices, one obvious next step on every screen.

05

Quiet Paper

One calm reading column. Services are written as the visitor's own problems, plus a three-step "how we work" section. The grid only shows at the page edges.
06

Choose Your Path

"What brings you here?" Four big choices, each showing a tailored answer and pre-filling the contact form. Includes a plain-language FAQ.
07

Split Panel

A fixed cyan grid panel on the left holds your name, the nav and the main button, and stays visible while the simple content scrolls on the right.

Inspired by alex-j-website_

08

Spec Sheet

That site's print style rebuilt around your cyan: a grid sheet inside a dark frame, chunky uppercase headings with solid cyan bars on accent words, small [ bracket ] labels, a stats strip, viewfinder corners and a spotlight on hover, a dossier-style About card and a night mode. The copy stays plain and easy to read.
08 · v2

Spec Sheet, animated

The same page with motion: the header drops in on load, headings sharpen into focus, cyan bars wipe in, labels scramble, stats count up, image shutters slide open, the chevron strip moves, the "Open to work" stamp thumps onto the About card, and night mode switches with a curtain sweep.
08 · v3

Spec Sheet, card stack

v2 with the portrait swapped for three shuffleable cards: an ID card, Industries I've built for, and Why work with me. Click, tap or press Enter to send the front card to the back.
